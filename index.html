LIMITED DROP · FOR DAMENSCH ONLY

The Ishani Drop.

Three ideas I’d bring to week one, one for each of the KRAs where a founder’s office earns its keep. They’re built from the outside in, from public information and how I shop, so they’re hypotheses to test with your data, not promises.

INFLUENCER MARKETING

#OneFullDay

Comfort is hard to show in a photo, so show it over time.

Creators wear one pair from 7 am to 11 pm on a real day: gym, commute, a nine-hour desk day, a humid evening out. The video is time-stamped, and the last shot is the honest verdict.

Mix
A few macro creators for reach, 40–50 micro creators (fitness, office life, college) for trust, and 150+ customers seeded for reviews and UGC.
Paid
Whitelist the best-performing videos as ads, so creator content does double duty.
Measure
A code and link per creator. CAC by tier, and the repeat rate of creator-acquired customers, not just views.
My own creator reel ↗

NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

The Gift Box line

A hypothesis worth one survey question: who is actually buying?

Many men’s basics are bought by someone else: a partner, a sibling, a parent. Ask “Who did you buy this for?” after checkout. If the share is real, it’s a segment no one is speaking to.

Product
Ready-to-gift boxes of three pieces and a note card, timed to Diwali, Valentine’s Day, Raksha Bandhan and Father’s Day, with an easy size exchange so the gifter isn’t scared of guessing wrong.
Why me
Slay Pop Cult was a gifting brand. I picked its assortment from what 350+ customers bought and ran it near 50% margin.
Gate
One box, one festival. Scale only if gift orders reach a set share and exchanges stay low.

GROWTH & EXECUTION

Three tests, three stop rules

Small bets with one metric each.

  1. Fit finder on product pages. Three questions (height, weight, preferred fit) suggest a size. Watch size-related returns and page-to-cart rate.
  2. The refill nudge. Basics wear out. A reminder around month six, with a refill pack. Watch repeat rate at 180 days.
  3. A bundle ladder. A clear step from 3-pack to 5-pack in the cart. Watch order value without losing conversion.

Each test gets a stop rule before it starts, the same discipline as the 35% repeat gate I set for Prolectro.

My first 90 days in the Founder’s Office

  1. WEEKS 1–4Listen

    Sit in on customer service, read 500 reviews and returns notes, and map the funnel from first ad to second order. Write down the three biggest leaks and bring them to the founder with numbers.

  2. WEEKS 5–8Test

    Run two or three of the tests above, each with one owner, one metric and a stop rule. Brief the first creator cohort for #OneFullDay.

  3. WEEKS 9–12Make it repeatable

    Turn what worked into playbooks and a weekly founder dashboard: CAC by channel, repeat rate, size returns and creator ROI.

A plan to test, not a promise. No internal DaMENSCH data was used.
