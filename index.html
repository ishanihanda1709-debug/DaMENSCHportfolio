IDEAS I’D BRING TO WEEK ONE

IDEA · INFLUENCER MARKETING

#OneFullDay.

Comfort is hard to show in a photo, so show it over time. Creators wear one pair from 7 am to 11 pm on a real day: gym, commute, a nine-hour desk day, a humid evening out, with an honest verdict at the end. A few macro creators for reach, 40–50 micro creators for trust, and customers seeded for reviews. The best videos run as paid ads.

Codes per creatorCAC by tierRepeat rate
IDEA · NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Who is actually buying?

Many men’s basics may be bought by someone else: a partner, a sibling, a parent. One question after checkout, “Who did you buy this for?”, would tell us. If the share is real, test a ready-to-gift box for one festival, with an easy size exchange. Slay Pop Cult was a gifting brand, so I’ve seen how gifting buyers behave.

1 survey question1 box, 1 festival
IDEA · GROWTH & EXECUTION

Three tests, three stop rules.

A fit finder on product pages (height, weight, preferred fit), watching size returns. A refill nudge around month six, watching repeat rate at 180 days. A clear step from 3-pack to 5-pack in the cart, watching order value without losing conversion. Each test gets a stop rule before it starts.

Fit finderRefill nudgeBundle ladder
MY FIRST 90 DAYS

Listen, test, make it repeatable.

Weeks 1–4: sit in on customer service, read 500 reviews and return notes, and map the funnel from first ad to second order. Weeks 5–8: run two or three of these tests, each with one owner and one metric. Weeks 9–12: turn what worked into playbooks and a weekly founder dashboard.

Weeks 1–4 listen5–8 test9–12 repeat

Ideas built from the outside in, from public information and how I shop. They’re hypotheses to test with DaMENSCH’s data, not promises.
